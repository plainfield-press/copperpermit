CopperPermit groups Austin's public electrical permits by job type, including pool work, battery storage, EV chargers and panel upgrades. Designed for suppliers, equipment dealers and contractors who want to see what work has been permitted.

The public digest is free to read. Open the daily feed or read the November 2-8 sample (440 permits). Public files omit homeowner names, street addresses and utility identifiers; geography is ZIP-level.
